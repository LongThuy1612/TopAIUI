# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

TopAICollection ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use %SITE_URL%, how we use it, and the choices you have.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address, your name and your password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we cannot read them.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture from Google.

We also record whether your email address has been confirmed.

### 1.2 Billing information

When you buy a service or subscribe to a plan, you may give us invoice details: company name, country, phone number, address, city, state or province, postal code and VAT number.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never receive or store full card numbers. We keep records of your orders, subscriptions and invoices, and the customer identifier Stripe gives us.

### 1.3 Content you submit

We store the content you send us, for example:

- Tool and GPT listings, logos and screenshots
- Reviews and ratings
- Articles, guest posts and link inserts
- Feedback messages and their attachments, with your browser's language and user agent to help us reproduce problems
- Contact form messages
- Social Listening queries and Website Insights exports

### 1.4 Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, our servers process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

When you see or click a sponsored listing, we record the impression or click so advertisers are charged correctly. For clicks we keep only a hashed form of your IP address, never the address itself.

We do not use analytics or advertising tracking services.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Provide the website and the features of your account
- Process payments, issue invoices and manage subscriptions
- Send service emails: email confirmation, password reset and replies to your messages
- Moderate submitted content and reviews
- Count advertising impressions and clicks and prevent click fraud
- Answer support requests and feedback
- Protect the website, our users and ourselves from abuse and security threats
- Meet legal and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails without your consent.

---

## 3. Cookies and browser storage

We use a cookie to remember your language. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local or session storage, and some features (such as review drafts) save data there too. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details.

---

## 4. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the website:

- **Stripe**: payment processing and invoicing
- **Google**: Sign in with Google, if you use it
- **Our email delivery provider**: sending service emails
- **Our hosting provider**: running the website and storing data

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only for the services they provide to us.

Public content you submit, such as reviews and tool listings, is visible to everyone. Reviews show your name.

## 6. External links

TopAICollection links to third-party tools and websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

## 7. Data security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords, hashed one-time links and restricted access to stored data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

When an account is deleted, we remove its sign-in details and release its email address. Order, payment and invoice records are kept as long as required for accounting and legal purposes. Content you published, such as reviews, may remain on the website without your account details.

- Ask us to delete your information
