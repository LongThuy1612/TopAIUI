# Editorial Guidelines

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

At TopAICollection, our editorial process aims to make every listing accurate, useful and relevant. These guidelines explain how we discover, evaluate, categorise and maintain the AI tools and GPTs on the website.

---

## 1. Our editorial mission

We want to be a reliable, well-organised directory that helps people find the right AI tool quickly.

We focus on:

1. Clarity over quantity
2. Usefulness over promotion
3. Accuracy over speed
4. User value over commercial influence

---

## 2. How tools are added

Tools reach the directory in two ways:

1. **Editorial discovery.** Our team adds tools found through research, public product directories, launch platforms and user demand. This costs the tool maker nothing.
2. **Paid submission.** Makers can submit a tool through our Submit service for a one-time fee. Payment covers processing, publication within 48 hours, a permanent listing and a highlighted card. It does not buy a positive description or rating.

A tool is considered for the directory if it:

1. Provides a clear AI-powered feature or workflow
2. Solves a specific problem or use case
3. Is publicly available and actively maintained
4. Has a working website

---

## 3. Evaluation criteria

We look at each tool using the same criteria:

- **Functionality**: does it do what it claims, reliably?
- **Use-case value**: how useful is it in real work such as writing, design, development, marketing or research?
- **User experience**: ease of use, clarity, onboarding and accessibility
- **Innovation**: new approaches or capabilities compared with existing tools
- **Reliability**: stability and consistency, where information is available

---

## 4. Categorisation

Tools are organised into categories and tags based on their primary function, target users and use cases, so they are easy to find. A tool can belong to several relevant categories.

---

## 5. Accuracy and updates

We keep listings accurate by:

1. Refreshing tool information and traffic data regularly
2. Updating descriptions when features or pricing change
3. Accepting update requests from tool makers, which we review before publishing
4. Removing or marking tools that are discontinued

The AI industry moves fast, so some information may change between updates.

---

## 6. Independence

Our descriptions are written to inform, not to promote.

1. Listing descriptions are not written by advertisers
2. Sponsored placements are labelled as ads
3. Paid submissions are shown with a highlighted card, which marks them as paid listings
4. Rankings are based on traffic data and are not for sale

---

## 7. Submissions and update requests

Tool makers may submit a tool or request changes to an existing listing. We may edit submitted information for clarity, accuracy, structure and consistency, and translate it into the languages we support, before publishing.

---

## 8. User reviews

Reviews come from signed-in users. Each user can have one active review per tool, and every review is checked by our team before it appears. We reject reviews that are spam, abusive, off-topic or clearly written by the tool's maker or competitors.

---

## 9. Removal policy

We may remove or update a listing if:

1. The tool is no longer available or maintained
2. Its information is outdated or inaccurate
3. It breaks the law or our standards
4. It is misleading or harmful to users

---

## 10. Fact-checking

We verify information against official product websites, public documentation, announcements and changelogs, and feedback from users. We do not publish user-submitted claims without checking them.

---

## 11. Changes to these guidelines

We may update these guidelines as our process or the AI industry changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
