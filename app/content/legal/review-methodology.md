# Review Methodology

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

This page explains how TopAICollection evaluates, compares and organises the AI tools on the website, so you know what the information on each page is based on.

---

## 1. Our purpose

Our methodology aims to show you clearly:

1. How tools are selected
2. How they are evaluated
3. How rankings and ratings are produced
4. How we keep the process fair and consistent

---

## 2. Data sources

We gather information from:

1. Official product websites
2. Documentation, changelogs and release notes
3. Public announcements and launch platforms
4. Third-party website traffic estimates
5. Reviews and feedback from our users
6. Hands-on testing where possible

We prefer primary sources whenever they are available.

---

## 3. Evaluation framework

Our team reviews each tool against five dimensions:

- **Functionality**: feature completeness, output quality and reliability
- **Ease of use**: onboarding, interface clarity and learning curve
- **Performance and stability**: speed, stability and error frequency
- **Value for the use case**: how well it solves real problems for its audience
- **Innovation**: how it differs from existing solutions

This assessment shapes how we describe and categorise a tool. We do not publish a numeric editorial score.

---

## 4. Ratings

The star rating on a tool's page is the average of approved user reviews. It is not set by our team and cannot be bought.

---

## 5. Rankings

Our ranking pages order tools by estimated monthly website traffic, and by traffic growth over one, three and six months. The data comes from third-party traffic estimates and is refreshed regularly.

To keep growth rankings meaningful, a tool needs a minimum amount of traffic before its growth is ranked.

Other lists on the website (newest tools, categories and search results) are sorted by recency, relevance and popularity, as shown on each page.

---

## 6. Review process

1. **Data collection**: we gather information from official and public sources
2. **Screening**: we check that the tool meets our inclusion criteria
3. **Evaluation**: we assess it using the framework above
4. **Categorisation**: we assign the most relevant categories and tags
5. **Publication**: the listing is published and translated
6. **Periodic review**: we revisit listings to keep them accurate

---

## 7. User review moderation

Only signed-in users can write reviews, one per tool. Every review is moderated before it is published, and we remove reviews that are spam, abusive, misleading or written with a conflict of interest.

---

## 8. Independence and sponsored content

1. Sponsored placements are labelled as ads
2. Paid submissions are shown with a highlighted card
3. Payment does not affect a tool's description, user rating or traffic ranking
4. All tools are assessed with the same framework

---

## 9. Limitations

1. AI tools change quickly and may change after we review them
2. Not every tool can be tested in depth
3. Traffic data is an estimate, not an exact measurement
4. Your experience may differ depending on your use case
